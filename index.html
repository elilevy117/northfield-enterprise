Company Overview

Northfield Enterprise is a family-owned company focused on building, supporting, and enduring businesses.

We believe strong businesses are built through care, consistency, and responsibility to the people involved, the work itself, and the long view. As the parent company of Northfield Mills™, NorthTek Industries, Kinderlac, Leighs of London, London Bros. and Leabridge Associates, our role is to provide steady guidance, thoughtful support, and the structure businesses need to grow with confidence and integrity.

Our approach is deliberate, disciplined, and built for durability.
